This document outlines the standard terms, services, and legally binding conditions for any sponsorships, product integrations, or long-term partnerships exclusively for the MarkedExiled YouTube channel.

1. Services & Pricing

1.1. Sponsorship Section

  • Service: A dedicated short section within an upcoming video advertising your service. The integration will delve into why audiences should use your service, utilizing proven marketing methods and extensive content creation experience.
  • Deliverables: In-video integration, 1 link in the top of the description, and 1 pinned comment.
  • Pricing: €1,500 – €10,000 (depending on business size and monthly/yearly earnings).

1.2. Product Video

  • Service: A full, dedicated video (standard ~10 minutes) focused entirely on your product. This includes a detailed review, unboxing, and relevant tests to showcase why audiences should purchase/use it.
  • Deliverables: Dedicated full-length video, 1 link in the description, and 1 pinned comment.
  • Pricing: €2,500 – €5,000 (depending on the product/company).
  • Note: The sponsor is responsible for sending the product and covering all shipping costs upfront.

1.3. Video Production Services

  • Service: A full video documenting an event you are hosting, a server creation, a plugin, etc. The primary mission is to craft a high-quality cinematic story from the subject matter.
  • Deliverables: Full cinematic video, 3 reserved links in the description (e.g., website, Discord server, socials), and a pinned comment.
  • Pricing: €1,800 per hour of final video runtime, billed against the runtime of the delivered video rather than hours spent in production and pro-rated for partial hours.

1.4. Partnerships

Long-term collaborations, available only if the services provide ongoing, mutual value. Example structures include:

  • Custom PC Manufacturers: The sponsor provides their latest workstation PC at negotiated intervals to continuously upgrade and accelerate the production environment.
  • Hardware/Peripheral Brands: The sponsor provides their latest components (e.g., monitors, microphones, studio gear).
  • Software Companies: Access to the software is provided free of charge, including free updates for the duration of the partnership.
  • Merchandise Companies: The sponsor designs and creates merchandise; profits are split based on an agreed-upon percentage.
